# Subject-to-Skill-to-Career Mapping

## Mathematics

Strong mathematics skills indicate aptitude for:
- Logical and analytical thinking
- Abstract problem solving
- Pattern recognition

Career alignment: Engineering (JEE), Data Science, Actuarial Science, Economics, Finance (CA/CMA), Architecture (NATA).
Students scoring above 80 in Mathematics should be encouraged toward competitive exam preparation.
Students scoring below 50 in Mathematics but strong elsewhere should explore Commerce or Humanities pathways.

## Science (Physics + Chemistry + Biology)

Strong Physics + Chemistry without Biology → Engineering pathway (JEE)
Strong Biology + Chemistry without Physics → Medicine pathway (NEET)
Strong all three → Exceptional versatility; consider IISc, IISER programs
Weak Science overall → Direct toward Commerce, Humanities, Design, or Sports

## English

Strong English performance indicates:
- Communication and writing skills
- Analytical reading ability
- Verbal reasoning strength

Career alignment: Law (CLAT), Journalism (IIMC), Civil Services (UPSC), MBA, Literature, Content Creation.
Students with high English scores should be encouraged toward debate, writing clubs, and MUN activities.

## Hindi and Regional Languages

Strong performance indicates cultural literacy and communication depth.
Teaching, translation, regional journalism, and civil services regional language papers benefit from this.
Students strong in Hindi can pursue Hindi literature, mass media in regional languages.

## Social Science (History, Geography, Political Science, Economics)

Strong Social Science students are natural candidates for:
- UPSC Civil Services
- Teaching (B.Ed + CTET)
- Law (CLAT)
- International relations and diplomacy
- NGO and development sector work
- Journalism and political commentary

## Extracurricular to Career Mapping

Cricket / Football / Badminton / Athletics → Sports careers, NDA, Physical Education (B.P.Ed)
Debate / MUN / Public Speaking → Law, Civil Services, Politics, Journalism, Corporate Leadership
Painting / Art / Sketching / Photography → Design careers (NIFT, NID, UCEED), Animation, Advertising
Music / Drama / Theatre → Performing arts, Film production, Media, Event Management
Science Olympiad / Math Club / Quiz → Research, IIT, IISc, IISER pathways, Competitive exams
Student Council / NCC → Civil Services, Defence, Management, Leadership roles
Environmental Club / Gardening → Agriculture, Environmental Science, Sustainability careers
Chess / Strategy Games → Data Analysis, Finance, Software Architecture, Game Design

## Learning Style to Career Fit

Visual learners → Design, Architecture, Data Visualization, Film, Animation
Auditory learners → Music, Law, Teaching, Counseling, Radio/Podcast, Languages
Kinesthetic learners → Medicine (surgery), Sports, Mechanical Engineering, Physical Therapy, Dance
Read-Write learners → Research, Writing, Journalism, Law, Civil Services, Publishing
