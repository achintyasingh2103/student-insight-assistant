# Indian Career Pathways Knowledge Base

## Engineering & Technology

Students strong in Mathematics and Science can pursue engineering via JEE Main and JEE Advanced.
IITs, NITs, and IIITs are premier engineering institutions in India.
Key branches: Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical Engineering.
Students interested in coding and problem-solving should practice competitive programming on Codeforces and LeetCode.
Skills to develop: logical reasoning, mathematics, physics fundamentals, programming (Python, C++).

GATE exam is required for postgraduate engineering admissions and PSU jobs.
Software engineering, data science, AI/ML, and cybersecurity are high-growth tech careers.
Students should start with Python programming and build projects on GitHub.

## Medicine & Healthcare

NEET-UG is the gateway to MBBS, BDS, BAMS, and other medical undergraduate courses.
AIIMS, JIPMER, and state medical colleges are top destinations.
Medicine requires strong Biology and Chemistry performance in Class 11 and 12.
Allied health careers: Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Nursing, Medical Lab Technology.
Students interested in healthcare but not medicine can explore Pharmacy (GPAT), Optometry, and Nutrition.

## Commerce & Finance

CA (Chartered Accountancy) through ICAI is one of India's most prestigious commerce careers.
CMA and CS are alternative professional qualifications in commerce.
B.Com, BBA, and MBA are common academic routes.
Students strong in Accountancy and Business Studies should pursue these.
Career options: Investment Banking, Financial Analysis, Auditing, Corporate Law, Actuarial Science.
CLAT is the entrance exam for National Law Universities — suits students strong in English and reasoning.

## Design & Creative Arts

NIFT (National Institute of Fashion Technology) entrance exam for fashion design.
NID (National Institute of Design) for product, graphic, and communication design.
UCEED for IIT design programs.
Students with strong art skills, creativity, and visual thinking are well-suited.
Career options: UI/UX Design, Architecture, Animation, Film Direction, Illustration, Interior Design.
Architecture requires NATA entrance exam.
Students showing aptitude in drawing, photography, or drama should explore these paths early.

## Social Sciences & Humanities

UPSC Civil Services is India's most competitive exam for IAS, IPS, and IFS officers.
Students strong in History, Geography, Political Science, and General Awareness are well-positioned.
Teaching is a respected career — B.Ed followed by CTET/state TET exams.
Journalism and Mass Communication: IIMC entrance exam.
Psychology, Sociology, and Economics offer careers in research, NGOs, policy, and counseling.
Students interested in social change should explore public policy, international relations, and development studies.

## Sports & Physical Education

NDA (National Defence Academy) entrance for Army, Navy, and Air Force — requires fitness and academic aptitude.
Sports quota admissions are available in many universities.
Students excelling in cricket, football, badminton, athletics should pursue district and state-level competitions early.
Sports Science, Physical Education (B.P.Ed), and Coaching certifications are formal career pathways.
Yoga instruction and fitness training are emerging career options.

## Agriculture & Environmental Sciences

Students from rural backgrounds or interested in nature can explore B.Sc Agriculture through state agricultural entrance exams.
ICAR universities offer excellent programs.
Environmental Science, Forestry, and Wildlife Management are growing fields.
Career options: Agricultural Officer, Soil Scientist, Environmental Consultant, Food Technologist.

## Entrepreneurship

No single entrance exam — entrepreneurship is a mindset developed through skills.
Students showing leadership, creativity, and business acumen should participate in school business competitions.
IIMs offer entrepreneurship programs at postgraduate level.
Startup India and MSME schemes support young entrepreneurs.
Skills to develop: communication, financial literacy, problem-solving, sales, and digital marketing.
